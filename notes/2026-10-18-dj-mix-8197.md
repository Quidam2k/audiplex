## P3 scope: phone track crossfade (not built)
- Add a second ExoPlayer ("outgoing") in PlaybackService, built with its own renderers and the same StreamCache source. At T-crossfade, the outgoing player takes over the *current* item at its position and keeps playing it. The main player (the one the session is pinned to) seeks to the next item and fades in. The outgoing player fades out and is released, with an equal-power ramp through each player's own ClipEdgeSink-style multiplier.
- Why this shape: MediaSession, queue indices (#3601 placeholders), DJ reporting and position sync all stay on the main player. Only the outgoing player is invisible.
- Rules, mirroring PC #2806: track -> track only. Never books, streams, clips, a paused player or a running sleep fade. Skip, pause or a new queue drops the outgoing player at once. A setting, off by default, sent via the existing `set_crossfade` command (the phone acks unknown_type today).
- Same mechanism gives DJ talk-over: play the clip on the outgoing player while the main player's song ducks to about 30%.
- Risks: the second decoder costs battery. Audio focus must stay with one player. Cache reads for two items at once.
- Estimate: about 250 lines plus JVM tests for the hand-off state machine. Ship in the same post-trip APK if it's ready, otherwise the one after.
