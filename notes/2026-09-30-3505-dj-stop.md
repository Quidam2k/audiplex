# #3505: DJ music wouldn't stop (2026-09-30) — diagnosis, fix, deploy notes

## What happened (verified from the live :8100 command registry)
- 00:28:48Z the DJ called `dj_sleep_timer(2.68 min, fade 6 s)`. The phone acked **failed: unknown_type 'sleep_timer'**.
- The phone runs an app build from around 2026-08-21 (the last package update it reported), older than the sleep engine (f99cc18, Sep 7). It also lacks `replace_upcoming`. **The published 1.0.48 was never installed.**
- `dj_sleep_timer` was fire-and-forget. It returned "Queued sleep timer" without reading the ack.
- The DJ personas appended one `queue` per song (00:13–00:31). The pool was inactive.
- The music stopped at 00:32:57Z via a DJ `pause`.
- Missing now-playing UI: current source already hydrates on connect (e093966). The phone predates or is at that build. Gaps that remained: `connect()` was a no-op once a controller existed, and the mini-player needed local state.

## Fix (commit a58407f4, branch duck-deeper)
- `server/audiplex/scheduled_stop.py`: server-run stop jobs, verified by the phone reporting playing=no.
  - `after_current`: trim the queue tail; if that isn't acked ok, pause ~1 s before the song's end; if the song changed first, pause now.
  - `fade`: volume steps, then pause, then restore the volume.
  - Stop latch: `/command` refuses queue/play_next with 409 "STOPPED: …" and the pool doesn't top up. It is lifted by play_now/resume/play_stream/play_book/activate or DELETE, and auto-expires after 3 h.
- API: `POST/GET/DELETE /api/playback/scheduled-stop`. GET `/state` has `stop` and passes through `app_version_name/code`.
- MCP:
  - New `dj_stop_after_current`, `dj_stop_status`, `dj_stop_cancel`.
  - `dj_sleep_timer` checks the ack and falls back to the server fade.
  - pause/skip/volume/seek/previous/reorder/bed_* say DONE only on an ok ack, else NOT DONE.
  - `dj_now_playing` shows the stop/latch reason and the phone's app version.
- Android held 1.0.49 (`data/held/3505/`, sha256 c965c833…e667d):
  - Mini-player falls back to the controller's metadata.
  - `refreshFromController()` on resume.
  - App version on /state.
  - `no_media_notification` diag event.
